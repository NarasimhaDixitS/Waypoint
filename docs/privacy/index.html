Privacy Policy

Waypoint for iPhone · Last updated 5 October 2026

Waypoint does not collect your data. Every task, goal, focus session and note you create is stored in a database on your own device. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing is uploaded.

What stays on your device

All of it. Specifically:

  • Tasks, goals, notes, schedules and repeat patterns
  • Your completion history, focus sessions and progress statistics
  • Your settings — theme, accent colour, reminder preferences

This information never leaves your iPhone unless you move it yourself, for example through an encrypted iPhone backup you choose to make. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, email addresses or passwords
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party trackers of any kind
  • Nothing is ever sold or shared, because nothing is ever collected

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by your iPhone. Waypoint hands them to iOS on your device; they are not sent through any server, and we are not told whether one was delivered or dismissed.

Subscriptions

Waypoint offers an optional subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple — we never see your name, your Apple ID or your payment details.

To know whether a subscription is active, Waypoint uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated on your device and the purchase information Apple provides, such as which product was bought and when it renews. It does not receive your name, email address, Apple ID or payment details. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Waypoint is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone of any age.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Because everything lives on your device, removing Waypoint removes all of it permanently. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to send us.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you. Waypoint holds none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. Your data is already entirely in your hands, on your own device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a revised date above. If a change ever means Waypoint starts collecting something, you will be told inside the app before it happens.
